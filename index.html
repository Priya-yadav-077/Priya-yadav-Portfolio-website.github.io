Selected work

Attention Mechanism Study — GLA vs. Linformer vs. Transformer
PyTorch, nanoGPT

Implemented and benchmarked three attention mechanisms — standard/FlashAttention, Linformer, and Gated Linear Attention (GLA) — inside Karpathy's nanoGPT (124M params) on character-level Shakespeare. The standard baseline generalized normally; Linformer's dramatically lower loss turned out to be overfitting on the small dataset rather than a genuine improvement.

GLA diverged to NaN; I traced this to an FP16 precision failure in its cumulative log-gate term, confirming the paper's own claim that hardware-aware CUDA kernels — out of scope for this assignment — are required to train it stably.
